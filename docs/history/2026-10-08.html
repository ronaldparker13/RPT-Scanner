How to read it

EP = gap on volume that held (buy the opening-range high next session, stop the day's low). HVC = high-volume close near the high (buy the hold, stop the low). SECOND CHANCE = price back at a recent EP/HVC close on quiet volume (buy the reclaim, stop under the level). BREAKOUT READY = 30%+ run, tight base, within 5% of the base high (watch for the close through it). BREAKOUT = closed through the base high on volume today. ★ = in a top-10 group. Level = the price that matters; Stop = low/high of the day or just past the level; Risk = distance to the stop. Nothing here is a recommendation; it is a list of charts to open.
